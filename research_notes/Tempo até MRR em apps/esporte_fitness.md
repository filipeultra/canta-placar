# Growth and revenue timelines of sports and fitness apps (racket sports and amateur-athlete stats)

> Method note: every figure below comes from web search results gathered on 2026-10-14. Full-page fetches were blocked by the network proxy for most domains (athletechnews, sacra, the5krunner, siliconangle, bikeradar, substack), so the numbers come from search-result summaries of the cited pages. They have not been checked against the full text. Labels: **[CO]** = company-reported (press release, CEO, own site); **[EST]** = third-party estimate (Sacra, Latka, CB Insights, Business of Apps, blogs); **[MEDIA]** = journalist reporting citing sources.

## Strava: founding, premium launch, subscribers/revenue by year, profitability, paid share

### Takeaway
Strava was founded in 2009. It sold a paid tier from at least the early 2010s (Premium, US$59/yr or US$7.99/mo by 2018) and split it into "Summit" packs in 2018. In May 2020 it went back to one subscription and moved leaderboards and routes behind the paywall. Its ARR is now around US$400–500M, almost all from subscriptions. In Dec 2023 the company said it "has operated profitably each year" over the prior few years. The paying share has always been small: about 2–3% of registered users by outside estimates, though the company claimed 20% in 2013. It has never published a subscriber count.

### Cited Findings
**Founding and product timeline**
- Founded 2009 in San Francisco; it started as an app for cycling enthusiasts [CO/EST] — [thepricer.org](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-does-strava-cost/); [Sacra via search](https://sacra.com/c/strava/)
- Pre-2018 Premium price: US$59/year or US$7.99/month (US); £5.99/month or £44.99/year (UK) — [DC Rainmaker, Aug 2018](https://dcrainmaker.com/2018/08/renames-premium-changes.html); [Trusted Reviews](https://www.trustedreviews.com/news/strava-summit-price-features-3521587)
- Aug 2018: Premium renamed "Summit" and split into 3 packs at US$2.99/mo (or US$23.99/yr) each. The full bundle stayed at US$7.99/mo — [DC Rainmaker](https://dcrainmaker.com/2018/08/renames-premium-changes.html); [road.cc](https://road.cc/content/tech-news/246070-strava-summit-replaces-premium-choice-three-packs-suit-your-cycling-needs)
- May 18, 2020: Summit folded back into one subscription, and segment leaderboards and route building moved behind the paywall. The founders framed the move as the "route for the company... to become profitable". UK price rose from £5.99 to £6.99/mo — [BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/strava-leaderboards-routes-subscription/); [road.cc forum](https://road.cc/?p=28790)
- The exact launch date of the original Premium is not confirmed by any retrieved source. See Gaps.
- Current US price: US$11.99/month or US$79.99/year; Family plan US$139.99/yr (2025–2026) [EST, aggregator] — [recurdash](https://recurdash.com/subscription-pricing/strava); [thepricer.org](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-does-strava-cost/)

**Users**
- Feb 2020: 50M accounts; late 2020: 68M (Bloomberg). The company said it was adding more than 2M athletes per month in 2020 [MEDIA/CO] — [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2020/11/16/strava-not-sold/); [cyclingindustry.news](https://cyclingindustry.news/strava-secures-110-million-in-series-f-financing)
- Nov 2020: "more than 70 million members in 195 countries" [CO] — [endurance.biz](https://endurance.biz/2020/industry-news/strava-secures-us110m-financing-in-partnership-with-tcv-and-sequoia/)
- Dec 2023: "grown from 50 million to over 120 million", over 400 employees [CO] — [Strava press](https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-appoints-google-executive-michael-martin-as-ceo)
- May 2025: more than 150M registered users; "over 50 percent growth in new users last year" [CO via WSJ] — [Built In SF](https://www.builtinsf.com/articles/strava-achieves-2b-valuation-20250523); [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/fitness-startup-funding-falters-strava-raise/)
- Feb 2026: "over 180 million athletes in more than 185 countries" [CO] — cited in [the5krunner IPO analysis](https://the5krunner.com/2026/01/09/strava-ipo-filing-3-billion-valuation-analysis/)

**Revenue and subscribers**
- Strava never discloses its paying-subscriber count — [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/01/09/strava-ipo-filing-3-billion-valuation-analysis/)
- About 90% of revenue comes from premium subscriptions (2023) [EST] — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/strava/)
- 2024 revenue US$338M; 2025 US$500M [EST, Latka] — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/strava). Business of Apps estimates 2025 at US$415M (+18.5%) [EST] — cited in [curvedtrading](https://curvedtrading.com/articles/en/investing/strava-ipo/). In Jan 2026 The Information reported revenue was believed to be below US$500M [MEDIA] — [curvedtrading](https://curvedtrading.com/articles/en/investing/strava-ipo/). **The sources conflict.**
- May 2025: CEO Michael Martin told the WSJ that Strava was "on track" to reach US$500M ARR [CO] — [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/fitness-startup-funding-falters-strava-raise/); [huddleup substack](https://huddleup.substack.com/p/inside-stravas-22-billion-ipo-how)
- Subscriber estimates: 1.2M–4M (blog analysis); 4–5M (forum post). Both are unverified [EST] — [the5krunner 2022](https://the5krunner.com/2022/05/25/strava-3-stories-plus-your-value-to-them-is-125/); [vitalmtb forum](https://www.vitalmtb.com/comment/809141)

**Paid share (conversion)**
- 2013: Strava told Outside that 20% of its users paid [CO, as reported second-hand] — [the5krunner 2020](https://the5krunner.com/2020/10/03/breaking-strava-needs-more-money-1-billion-valuation)
- 2015 sample: about 192k paying out of 8M (about 2.5%); 2019 sample of 5,000 users: 3.16% [EST, blogger sampling] — [the5krunner 2020](https://the5krunner.com/2020/10/03/breaking-strava-needs-more-money-1-billion-valuation)
- Premium penetration is about 2% of registered users [EST] — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/strava/)

**Profitability**
- 2019: "in its 10-year existence, Strava has yet to turn a profit" [MEDIA] — [FITT Insider 2019](https://insider.fitt.co/socializing-fitness-stravas-social-media/)
- 2020: the company said it was not yet profitable and needed to become so — [BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/strava-leaderboards-routes-subscription/)
- Dec 2023: "...the company has operated profitably each year" over the past few years [CO] — [Strava press release](https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-appoints-google-executive-michael-martin-as-ceo); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/strava-appoints-google-executive-michael-martin-as-ceo-302010620.html)
- A secondary source says Strava has been "profitable since 2021", citing Martin's May 2025 interview; other outlets say 2020. The year is unverified [EST] — [huddleup substack](https://huddleup.substack.com/p/inside-stravas-22-billion-ipo-how)

**Funding and IPO**
- Nov 2020: US$110M Series F led by TCV and Sequoia, at a valuation above US$1.5B — [cyclingindustry.news](https://cyclingindustry.news/strava-secures-110-million-in-series-f-financing); [Brazil Journal](https://braziljournal.com/em-nova-pedalada-strava-ja-vale-us-15-bi)
- May 2025: round led by Sequoia, size undisclosed and including debt, at a US$2.2B valuation. It followed the acquisitions of Runna and The Breakaway — [SGB](https://sgbonline.com/strava-nets-2-2-billion-valuation-in-funding-round/); [nasdaq/Crunchbase](https://nasdaq.com/articles/strava-valuation-powers-22b-fitness-startup-funding-falters-0)
- Jan 2026: confidential draft S-1 filed and Goldman Sachs hired (The Information). Strava confirmed the filing in Feb 2026. No public S-1 or audited figures had appeared as of Oct 2026 — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/01/08/strava-makes-confidential-ipo-filing-amid-subscription-revenue-growth/); [Forge](https://forgeglobal.com/insights/strava-upcoming-ipo-news/)

### Inferences
- Strava took about 11+ years (2009 to around 2020–21) to reach profitability. The trigger was a deliberate paywall tightening in 2020, when core social and competitive features (leaderboards, routes) went behind the paywall. It was not the original premium launch. The free social graph came first, and the network effect made the later paywall tolerable.
- With 150–180M registered users and a low single-digit paid share, about US$400–500M revenue suggests roughly 4–6M payers at about US$80/yr. This is a rough back-calculation, not a reported figure.
- The 20% paid claim in 2013 versus about 2–3% later suggests early adopters (serious cyclists) convert far better than the mass base that arrives later. The share of payers falls as the free base grows.

### Gaps
- The exact launch year and price of the original Strava Premium (believed to be early 2010s) were not confirmed. No source was retrieved.
- There is no company-reported revenue or subscriber count for any year. The first audited numbers will come only with a public S-1.
- No retrievable source dates Strava's 1k/10k/100k/1M-user milestones.

## Playtomic (padel/racket booking, Spain): users, revenue, funding, Brazil

### Takeaway
Playtomic was founded in 2017 in Madrid. It makes money mainly through booking fees and club SaaS (Playtomic Manager), with a player "Premium" subscription added later. By 2025 it reported €346M in transactions, €29M net revenue, about 4.7M players and 6,000+ clubs in 66 countries, and said it was growing "profitably". No source retrieved reports Brazil-specific numbers.

### Cited Findings
- Founded 2017 by Félix Ruiz, Pedro Clavería and Pablo Carro — [EU-Startups 2021](https://www.eu-startups.com/2021/12/madrid-based-playtomic-smashes-series-c-funding-with-a-e56-million-raise-for-its-tennis-and-padel-app/)
- Dec 2021: €56M Series C. At that time the company was "developing a premium subscription service aimed at power users" [CO/MEDIA] — [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2021/12/madrid-based-playtomic-smashes-series-c-funding-with-a-e56-million-raise-for-its-tennis-and-padel-app/)
- Jun 2023: acquired AirCourts (Portugal), saying the deal gave it about 87% of Portuguese clubs [CO] — [Padel Alto](https://padelalto.com/2023/06/15/playtomic-acquires-portuguese-competitor-raised-millions-in-latest-funding-round)
- 2024: more than €240M gross bookings (+40%), 6,000 clubs in 63 countries, 1.5M monthly active players [CO via Reuters] — [Bolsamania](https://www.bolsamania.com/noticias_amp/mercados/economiafinanzas--playtomic-cierra-una-ronda-de-65-millones-para-su-expansion-en-europa-y-estados-unidos--19173575.html); [Global Banking & Finance/Reuters](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/SPAIN-PLAYTOMIC-4d20e46b-4b4b-40cd-a211-6b328f17950d/)
- Mar 2025: €65M round (€55M equity led by Match Invest and LFH, plus a €10M Santander loan) at a reported €250M valuation (Reuters). Stated focus: US, UK, Germany and pickleball — [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/playtomic-secures-e65m/); [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/playtomic-raises-65m-for-us-expansion)
- Dec 2025: Crowdcube community round raised over €5.1M from 4,600+ players, clubs and fans. As of Sep 25, 2025: €346M transactions (+51% YoY), €29M net revenue (+38% YoY), 66 countries, growing "quickly and profitably" [CO] — [FITT press release](https://insider.fitt.co/press-release/playtomic-raises-over-e5-1-million-thanks-to-the-support-of-its-community/)
- More than 4.7M players and 6,000+ clubs in 66 countries (late 2025) [MEDIA] — [Emprendedores](https://emprendedores.es/startups/playtomic-cierra-ronda-inversion-en-la-que-implica-a-su-comunidad/). Its Portuguese-language site lists about 6,700 clubs, 25,000+ courts, 1.7M monthly active players and 4M+ registered users [CO] — [playtomic.com/pt](https://playtomic.com/pt)
- Player Premium plan (monthly or annual, billed through the app stores): no booking, match or cancellation fees; advanced stats (court time, win streaks, comparison with top performers); level-progression graphs; priority alerts; "Golden Matches". The price was not found in the sources [CO] — [Playtomic help](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20534670300561-Playtomic-Premium-Plan-for-players); [player help](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831696399633)
- Aggregator figures are unreliable. Preqin lists 2023 revenue of US$145.8M with EBITDA of -US$2.45M (likely gross bookings, not revenue). Latka's profile describes a different company. CB Insights estimates 2025 at US$33.8M [EST] — [Preqin](https://www.preqin.com/data/profile/asset/playtomic-s-l-/261425); [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/playtomic); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/playtomic/financials)
- Brazil: clubs are listed on the platform (e.g., Academia Santo Padel Nacional, São Paulo), and Playtomic is described as present in Latin America. No Brazil user or club counts were found — [Playtomic club page](https://playtomic.com/clubs/academia-santo-padel-nacional); [The Padel Weekly](https://thepadelweekly.com/playtomic-expands-its-reach-in-the-u-s-padel-scene-with-new-strategic-partnerships/)
- Apr 2025: global partnership with Babolat — [The Padel Weekly](https://thepadelweekly.com/babolat-and-playtomic-unite-to-drive-global-padel-expansion/)

### Inferences
- Net revenue is about 8.4% of transaction volume (€29M / €346M). The marketplace take rate, not player subscriptions, is the main revenue engine. Premium is an add-on whose main value is removing fees plus stats and level graphs.
- About 8 years from founding to roughly €30M net revenue, with "profitable" claimed by 2025. The club-side SaaS gave the B2B distribution, and players then came for court availability.
- Stats and level graphs are a paid hook in Playtomic Premium. That shows amateur racket players will pay for self-quantification, but bundled with a utility (no booking fees).

### Gaps
- No Brazil-specific figures (users, clubs, entry date). Brazilian outlets (Exame, Startups, Valor) gave no results in the searches.
- No Premium price, Premium subscriber count, or Premium launch date.
- No year-by-year user milestones before 2024.

## SwingVision (tennis AI video)

### Takeaway
SwingVision (Mangolytics Inc.) was founded in 2019. It is a small niche business: about US$1M ARR within roughly 2 years of the iPhone launch, more than 100k players by 2022, about US$10M raised, and an estimated US$2.5M revenue in 2024. Its channels are clubs, colleges and coach associations.

### Cited Findings
- Founded 2019 by Swupnil Sahai (ex-Tesla Autopilot) and Richard Hsu. An Apple Watch app came in 2016; the iPhone/iPad app launched in 2019 (some accounts say 2020) — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/swingvision-6-million-funding/amp/); [Athletech News](https://athletechnews.com/swingvision-redefining-tennis/); [Apple Developer Spotlight](https://apps.apple.com/ca/iphone/story/id1565190205)
- 2021: passed US$250k quarterly revenue; seed round of US$2M; the US was 45% of subscribers [MEDIA] — [Sportico 2022](https://www.sportico.com/leagues/tennis/2022/tennis-australias-vc-fund-follows-nfl-1234658483/)
- Apr 2022: passed a US$1M annual revenue run rate (in 2021), with 100M shots recorded by more than 100k players. US$2M raised from Wildcard Ventures, Sony Innovation Fund, Techstars and others, with focus on clubs and colleges [CO] — [TennisNerd](https://www.tennisnerd.net/press-releases/swingvision-raises-2-million-dollars/26870)
- Oct 2023: US$6M Series A led by Authentic Ventures, to expand into pickleball and padel, tournament officiating and remote coaching [CO] — [SGI Europe](https://www.sgieurope.com/ai-powered-tennis-app-swingvision-raises-6-million/105552.article); [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/swingvision-6-million-funding/)
- Used by more than 100 D1 college tennis teams [CO] — [Pickleball Union](https://pickleballunion.com/swingvision-brings-its-award-winning-ai-to-pickleball/)
- Nov 2024: partnership with the RSPA (coaches association) giving members a discount and early access to remote coaching [CO] — [RSPA](https://members.rspa.net/USPTA/About_USPTA/Who_We_Are/News/2024_News_Folder/RSPA%20and%20SwingVision%20Announce%20Partnership%20to%20Elevate%20the%20Coaching%20Game%20for%20Professionals.aspx)
- Total funding about US$10M over 4 rounds (Dealroom) or US$9.7M over 6 (CB Insights). 2024 revenue about US$2.5M [EST] — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/swingvision); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/swingvision/financials)
- Pricing: freemium plus Pro. One coach-promo page cites US$149.99/year as the value of Pro [CO-adjacent, promo] — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/swingvision); Apple podcast page (via search)

### Inferences
- From the 2019 iPhone launch to about US$1M ARR took roughly 2 years. Reaching about US$2.5M took roughly 5 years. A premium-priced (about US$150/yr) single-sport stats app with a strong AI moat stays a low-single-digit-millions business unless it expands to more sports or into B2B (colleges, clubs).
- 100k players with about US$1M ARR at about US$150/yr implies about 6–7k payers, or 6–7% of players. This is a rough inference, not reported.

### Gaps
- No current user or subscriber counts. The launch price of Pro and current list prices were not confirmed from primary pages.

## Other apps with public numbers (Hudl, PlaySight, Babolat Play/Zepp, DUPR, UTR, Runkeeper, MyFitnessPal, Whoop, Garmin Connect+, Ladder, Brazilian racket apps)

### Takeaway
The large outcomes came from very large free bases sold to strategics (MyFitnessPal, Runkeeper), B2B team subscriptions through coaches (Hudl), or hardware plus membership (Whoop). Racket-specific stats and rating apps (DUPR, UTR, SwingVision) stay small. Their best lever is becoming the "official" rating or stat layer for clubs and federations. Hardware-sensor plays (Babolat Play, Zepp) were discontinued.

### Inferences
- MyFitnessPal (10 years to about 80M users before monetizing with premium) and Runkeeper show that many fitness apps reached exits on user scale, not subscription revenue. That route is less available today, now that buyers value ARR.
- Ranken's numbers (500 active athletes, about 150 games/week after about 4 years) are a realistic early-stage benchmark for a Brazilian racket stats/ranking app. For comparison, DUPR reached 500k users in about 2.5 years because it became the de facto rating standard for a booming sport.

### Gaps
- No public numbers found for TennisKeeper, PadelMates, "BT Tracker", Nike Run Club (free; no revenue disclosed) or Future.
- No reported subscriber counts for Garmin Connect+, DUPR or UTR Power.
- Zepp tennis: no data retrieved.

## Typical free-to-paid conversion, ARPU and seasonality in fitness/sports apps

### Takeaway
Benchmarks (all third-party): about 2–5% of free users pay; about 3.8% median installs-to-paid for fitness; trial-to-paid about 35–40% median (top decile about 68%); longer trials (17–32 days) convert best. Fitness ARPU estimates range from about US$13 to US$26 per payer per year. Downloads spike strongly in January, but revenue builds through spring.

### Cited Findings
- Health & fitness median trial-to-paid 39.9%, top 10% at 68.3% (RevenueCat 2025). Trials of 17–32 days convert at 45.7% median vs 26.8% for trials of 4 days or less. iOS converts about 2x Android [EST] — [Athletech News](https://athletechnews.com/?p=124212); [RevenueCat 2025](https://revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)
- RevenueCat 2026: 42.5% median for 17–32 day trials [EST] — [SaaStr](https://saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps-how-115000-mobile-apps-deliver-16b-in-revenue-whats-working-whats-quietly-killing-growth)
- Adapty: health & fitness trial-to-paid 35.0% (highest category, but lowest first-renewal retention); a 2026 Adapty figure of 62% is also cited (methods differ). Median fitness installs-to-paid 3.8% (2025) [EST] — [Airbridge](https://www.airbridge.io/blog/hard-vs-soft-paywalls); [RocketShip HQ](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/?p=5592); [RocketShip HQ](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/?p=5390); [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/health-fitness-app-subscription-benchmarks/)
- Median trial start rate about 5–7% of installs for H&F (RevenueCat 2025, secondary) [EST] — [RocketShip HQ](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/?p=5565)
- General median free-to-paid 2–5%; fitness ARPU about US$13 [EST] — [Forasoft](https://www.forasoft.com/blog/article/app-revenue-potential). Alternative: US$25.78 [EST] — [Airbridge](https://www.airbridge.io/blog/mmp-conversion-fee-roi-at-scale)
- Strava-specific paid share: about 2–3% (estimates, see above) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/strava/); [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2020/10/03/breaking-strava-needs-more-money-1-billion-valuation)
- Seasonality: January is "the biggest month" for H&F; Jan 2021 installs were 277M (+21% YoY) [EST] — [Apptopia](https://apptopia.com/en/blog/health-fitness-app-performance-january-21-device-connected-apps-see-revenue-grow/). Jan 2025 H&F IAP revenue was a record US$385M (+10% YoY) [EST] — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-mobile-health-and-fitness-in-2025). US App Store data (2020–21) showed a download peak at New Year with no matching revenue peak; revenue grows through spring and plateaus by late summer [EST] — [AppTweak](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/close-up-on-trending-eu-health-fitness-apps)

### Inferences
- For an amateur racket stats app, a planning range is 2–5% of active users paying, or about 4% of installs with a well-built trial. Use a longer trial (about 2–4 weeks) so users log several matches before the paywall.
- Outdoor racket sports (beach tennis, padel) are likely tied to the weather and summer seasons. In Brazil, peak play falls in Dec–Mar, which may differ from the January New Year pattern in global data. This is an inference; no Brazil-specific data was found.

### Gaps
- No sports-specific (as opposed to general fitness) conversion benchmarks were found, and none for Brazil or LatAm pricing elasticity.

## Lessons on monetizing amateur-athlete communities (network effects, clubs/coaches, federations)

### Takeaway
The winners first built a free social or utility layer, then paywalled competitive and analytical features (Strava 2020). Or they monetized the B2B side (club SaaS, team subscriptions) and let players come for free (Playtomic, Hudl). Racket-stat players gain most by becoming the official rating or stat layer through clubs, coaches and federations (DUPR, UTR, SwingVision with colleges and RSPA).

### Cited Findings
- Strava's 2020 move of leaderboards and routes behind the paywall was explicitly framed as the path to profitability. Profitability was reported by 2023 — [BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/strava-leaderboards-routes-subscription/); [Strava press](https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-appoints-google-executive-michael-martin-as-ceo)
- Strava earns non-subscription revenue from sponsored challenges (about 10% of revenue) [EST] — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/strava/)
- Strava grew its US$2.2B valuation partly by buying training content (Runna, The Breakaway), adding paid value on top of the social graph — [SGB](https://sgbonline.com/strava-nets-2-2-billion-valuation-in-funding-round/)
- Playtomic: about 8.4% net take on €346M of transactions. The club network (6,000+ clubs) is the distribution channel. A community crowdfund raised €5.1M from 4,600+ players and clubs — [FITT press release](https://insider.fitt.co/press-release/playtomic-raises-over-e5-1-million-thanks-to-the-support-of-its-community/)
- Playtomic Premium monetizes stats, level graphs and promoted matches for players — [Playtomic help](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20534670300561-Playtomic-Premium-Plan-for-players)
- Hudl sells to teams and coaches (160k teams, 6M users in 2020): a B2B channel at youth and high-school level — [SGB](https://sgbonline.com/hudl-gains-investment-from-bain-capital/)
- SwingVision's channels: clubs, colleges (100+ D1 teams), and a coach-association partnership (RSPA, 2024) — [TennisNerd](https://www.tennisnerd.net/press-releases/swingvision-raises-2-million-dollars/26870); [Pickleball Union](https://pickleballunion.com/swingvision-brings-its-award-winning-ai-to-pickleball/); [RSPA](https://members.rspa.net/USPTA/About_USPTA/Who_We_Are/News/2024_News_Folder/RSPA%20and%20SwingVision%20Announce%20Partnership%20to%20Elevate%20the%20Coaching%20Game%20for%20Professionals.aspx)
- UTR invests in its own tournament circuit (more than US$11M in 2025) and sells Power subscriptions tied to event registration discounts. The rating plus tournaments form a loop — [UTR press](https://www.utrsports.net/blogs/press/utr-sports-announces-expanded-2025-global-calendar-11-million-pro-tennis-investment)
- DUPR grew to 500k users with 20% MoM growth by becoming the rating standard, and brought in a celebrity owner (Agassi) — [DUPR](https://dupr.com/post/andre-agassi-david-kass-and-raine-ventures-acquire-controlling-interest-in-dupr-invest-dollar8m)
- Garmin Connect+'s paywall on top of a free product drew a largely negative reaction. Putting a paywall on existing free value carries risk — [itwriting](https://www.itwriting.com/blog/date/2025/03); [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/04/20/garmin-connect-plus-review/)
- Ladder shows a paid-only, coach-led model can reach more than 50k payers in about 2 years and attract revenue-based marketing financing — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/ladder-12-million-funding/); [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2024/12/10/strength-training-ladder-app-users-growth)

### Inferences
- Time to meaningful MRR for social-stats apps is long. Strava took about 11 years to profitability, MyFitnessPal about 10 years before premium, and SwingVision about 2 years to US$1M ARR but stayed at a few million. Faster paths add a B2B payer (clubs, organizers) or a hard paywall from day one (Ladder).
- For a Brazilian amateur racket-stats app, the transferable plays are:
  - Free match logging plus a social feed, to build network effects.
  - Paywalled advanced stats, history and level graphs (the Playtomic Premium and UTR Power pattern).
  - Organizers, arenas and coaches as the channel and a possible payer.
  - Becoming the reference ranking for local tournaments and federations (the DUPR/UTR pattern).

### Gaps
- No source quantified how much clubs, coaches or federations contribute to conversion or retention for any of these apps.
- No data on the Brazilian beach tennis federation (CBT) or padel federation partnerships with apps.
